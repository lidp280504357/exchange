## Create an account

1. Open the [sign-up page](/register) and enter your email.
2. Pass the human check, click "Send code" and enter the 6-digit code from the email.
3. Choose a password, read and accept the terms and the risk disclosure, and finish.

You are signed in right away, and your spot account receives simulated funds (10,000 USDT, 0.1 BTC and 2 ETH), so you can start trading at once.

> SMS is simulated in the test environment and no real text messages arrive: sign up with an **email address**.

### Password rules

- At least 10 characters;
- not a common password, and no runs or repeats (such as `12345678` or `aaaaaaaa`);
- not containing your email.

### About codes

- A code works for 5 minutes; after 5 wrong tries, request a new one.
- One code per email every 60 seconds, at most 5 an hour and 10 a day.
- No email? Check the spam folder, then check the address you typed.

## Sign in

Normally you sign in with **your email and password**.

### After 7 days away

So that a leaked password alone cannot open your account, an account with **no successful sign-in for 7 days** needs one more step after the correct password:

1. The page asks for a code and shows where it goes (partly hidden).
2. Request the 6-digit code sent to **your bound email** (or your phone, if one is bound) and enter it.
3. You are signed in, and the 7 days start again.

### Failed attempts and locking

- After 3 wrong passwords in a row, each attempt needs the human check first.
- After 10, the account is locked for 15 minutes and then unlocks by itself.

### Forgot your password

Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page (or open [reset password](/reset)), get a code at your bound email and choose a new password. Afterwards:

- every device is signed out and has to sign in again;
- for safety, withdrawals in the next 24 hours are reviewed by a person.

## Staying signed in and devices

- A device stays signed in as long as you use it at least once every 30 days; after 30 days away you sign in again.
- An account can be signed in on up to 10 devices; beyond that the least recently used one is signed out and you are told.
- [Devices](/account/sessions) lists your devices and sign-in history, and signs out any you do not recognize.

The desktop and mobile sites keep separate sign-ins, so sign in on each.

> This site is a test environment for learning; every balance is simulated.
