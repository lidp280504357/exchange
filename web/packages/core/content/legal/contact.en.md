## How to reach us

The footer shows our support e-mail and help desk. When you write, include the e-mail or phone number of your account (never your password or codes), when the problem happened and a screenshot, so we can help sooner.

## Common questions

The [help centre](/help) answers many questions directly: deposits not credited, withdrawal reviews, authenticator apps and fees.

## Stay safe

We never ask for your password, one-time codes or your authenticator's secret. Anyone claiming to be support who asks for them is a scammer.
