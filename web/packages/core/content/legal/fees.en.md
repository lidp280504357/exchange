## Trading fees

- **Spot**: makers and takers pay a share of the trade's value; each market's trading page shows its rates.
- **Perpetual futures**: makers and takers pay their own rates on the position value traded; while a position is open, longs and shorts also exchange funding at the funding rate.

Fees are taken from the asset you receive when the trade fills; your order history and fund flow show each one.

## Deposits, withdrawals and transfers

- **Deposits**: free on the platform; the sender pays the network fee.
- **Withdrawals**: a fixed fee per network, shown on the withdrawal page once you pick the network.
- **Transfers**: free between your spot and futures accounts.

Fees may change with the markets; we announce changes in advance.
