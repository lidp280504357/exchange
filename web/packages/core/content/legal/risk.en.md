summary: Trading digital assets is high risk. Take part only when you understand the risks and can afford them.

## Price risk

Digital assets trade around the clock and their prices move sharply: they can rise or fall a lot in a short time, even to zero. Past performance says nothing about future returns.

## Futures and leverage

Perpetual futures use leverage, and results follow the full position value. When the market moves against you, your margin can run out fast and the position be liquidated; you can lose more than you expected. Set stop-losses and do not use more leverage than you can bear.

## Liquidity

When prices swing or the market is thin, orders may not fill at the price you expect, and market orders may slip a lot.

## Technology and networks

Network outages, device failures, maintenance or third-party problems can keep you from placing or canceling orders or seeing prices in time.

## Account security

Keep your password, one-time codes and authenticator app safe, and beware of phishing sites and fake support staff. We never ask for your password or codes.

## Regulation

Rules on digital assets differ between countries and change over time, and may affect which services can be offered.

Make sure you understand these risks and decide for yourself, in line with your own finances.
