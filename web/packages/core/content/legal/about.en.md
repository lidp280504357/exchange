## What we care about

- **Security**: a ledger where every entry can be traced and is reconciled regularly, a second check for sensitive actions, risk checks and limits on withdrawals.
- **Openness**: fees, rules and listings are published, and changes announced in advance.
- **Reliability**: the services are watched around the clock; incidents and maintenance are announced promptly.
