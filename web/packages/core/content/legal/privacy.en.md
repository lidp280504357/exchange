## What we collect

- **Account details**: your e-mail or phone number, your password (stored encrypted), whether an authenticator app is set up.
- **Security data**: sign-in times, IP addresses and devices, to spot unusual sign-ins and protect your account.
- **Trading and funds**: orders, trades, deposits, withdrawals and transfers.
- **Preferences**: language, time zone, theme and the like, some kept only on your device.

## How we use it

- To provide the services and settle trades and funds;
- To send one-time codes, security notices and necessary service messages;
- To detect and prevent fraud, account takeover and other risks;
- To meet legal and regulatory requirements.

## How we protect it

- Passwords and keys are stored encrypted; every connection uses HTTPS.
- E-mail addresses and phone numbers are masked in logs and analytics.
- Access to data is limited to what each task needs, and every access is audited.

## Sharing

We do not sell or give your personal data to others, except to the providers that deliver our e-mail and SMS (only what the delivery needs) and where the law, a court or a regulator requires it.

## Your rights

You can see and correct your details and your notification choices in your account settings, and contact us to close your account. Please move your assets out before you close it.

## Updates

We may update this policy as the services change; a new version applies once published on the platform.
