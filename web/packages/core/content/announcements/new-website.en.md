The Astras website has been rebuilt: desktops and phones no longer share one responsive page. Each gets an interface designed for it, on top of the same data and components.

## Two sites

| Site | Address | What it is like |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Desktop | astras.vip | Wide layouts, a multi-panel trading terminal, full market tables |
| Mobile | m.astras.vip | Portrait and touch first, bottom navigation, orders in a sheet, can be added to the home screen |

- Opening the desktop site on a phone takes you to the same page on the mobile site, and the other way round. Both sites use the same addresses, so links can be shared as they are.
- The footer's "Mobile site / Desktop site" switch keeps your choice and stops the redirect.
- Each site keeps its own sign-in, so sign in on each.

## What is new

- **Smoother live prices**: the whole site keeps one live connection that survives page changes, and prices and order books update in step with the screen.
- **Markets page**: categories, search, sortable columns and 7-day trends; favorites follow your account once you sign in.
- **Coin pages**: candles, 24-hour figures, an introduction and links to the website, block explorer and white paper.
- **Announcements and a help center**: this board, and help articles on signing up, security, deposits and withdrawals, spot trading and futures.
- **Languages and preferences**: every screen in Chinese and English; choose your time zone, the colors of rises and falls (green up or red up) and order confirmations in the settings.

Since the mobile site went live (2026-10-01) the previous interface at astras.vip/h5/ is retired; its links lead to the home page.
